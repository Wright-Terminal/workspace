About Wright Treo

Founded 2026

Wright Treo Limited builds market, finance, pricing and asset management infrastructure for aviation leasing. We started with a simple observation: the people pricing, placing and financing aircraft were doing it with less real-time market data than a retail equities trader — scattered PDFs, one-off spreadsheets, and news that arrived too late to act on.

Wright Terminal is our answer — a single terminal that brings fleet data, valuation, lease benchmarking and counterparty risk into one place, built for the people who actually run the desk.

The name is a small piece of wordplay: Treo is Gaelic for "direction" — put it after Wright and it reads as "the right direction," which is as much the idea behind the company as the name.

01

Real data, honestly labelled

Where public disclosure exists, we show it. Where it doesn't, we say so — never a fabricated number standing in for a gap.

02

Built for the whole desk

Lessor, operator, financier or investor — Wright Terminal adapts to how your account actually uses the market.

03

Private by default

Your fleet, your leases, your documents — scoped to your account and never visible to anyone else on the platform.
